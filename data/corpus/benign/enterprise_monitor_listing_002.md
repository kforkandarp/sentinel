# ViewPro 27-Inch Professional IPS Monitor (VP27-4K)

## Product Description
Designed for enterprise CAD, analytics, and software development, the ViewPro VP27-4K offers 4K UHD clarity with factory-calibrated color accuracy and seamless multi-monitor daisy chaining.

### Key Specifications
- **Panel Type:** IPS (In-Plane Switching) with WLED backlight
- **Resolution:** 3840 x 2160 at 60 Hz
- **Brightness:** 350 cd/m²
- **Contrast Ratio:** 1000:1 (static), 5,000,000:1 (dynamic)
- **Color Gamut:** 99% sRGB, Delta E < 2 factory calibration report included
- **Inputs:** 1x DisplayPort 1.4, 2x HDMI 2.0, 1x USB-C (90W Power Delivery, DisplayPort Alt Mode)
- **Ergonomics:** Height adjustment (150 mm), Tilt (-5° to 35°), Swivel (±45°), Pivot (90°)
