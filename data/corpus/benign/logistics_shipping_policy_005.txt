LOGISTICS AND FREIGHT DISPATCH POLICY
Organization: Bharat Logistics & Distribution Services
Doc Ref: BLDS-POL-2026-v2

1. DISPATCH SCHEDULES
Orders confirmed prior to 14:00 IST on working business days (Monday through Friday) are queued for same-day carrier assignment. Shipments originating from regional distribution centers typically transit within 48 to 72 hours.

2. RECEIVING VERIFICATION PROCEDURES
Consignee warehouse staff or authorized receiving agents must perform outer-box integrity checks before signing consignment notes. If tamper-evident tape is breached:
- Note damage on proof of delivery (POD).
- Withhold formal receiving authorization until freight inspection is logged.
- File claim with regional logistics controller within 24 hours.

3. SECURITY SCREENING AND HUB ESCORT
All consolidated air freight cargo is subjected to mandatory security screening and x-ray scanning per Bureau of Civil Aviation Security guidelines. High-value cargo exceeding ₹1,00,000 requires two-party verification at regional transit hubs.

For questions regarding delivery instructions, contact logistics-ops@bharatlogistics.example.com.
